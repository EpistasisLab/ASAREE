What ran and why
  • Nonnegative counts are compared using Poisson and overdispersed negative-binomial models.
  • Selected model: poisson. Factors: Approach, Setting. Interactions included through order 2.
  • Comparisons are equally weighted differences or ratios of predicted means; 95% confidence intervals.
  • Factor tests evaluate model terms; comparisons evaluate predicted overall means. These can answer different questions for nonlinear or multipart models.
  • Differences subtract the second listed condition from the first; ratios divide the first by the second. The null value is 0 for differences and 1 for ratios. Interactions compare effects across settings.
  • Holm correction is applied across factor tests and within each factor's comparison family. Separate metrics are not corrected together; cross-metric findings are exploratory.
  • Analytic and permutation results are reported independently; neither is selected based on significance.
  • Permutation p-values are separately Holm-adjusted working-scale OLS sensitivity checks, not permutations of the model's Wald statistic. Labels are exchanged within other-factor strata for main effects; interactions use approximate Freedman–Lane reduced-model residual permutation. These require exchangeability under the tested null, beyond independent runs. Nonlinear/multipart models and rank-based ordinal checks may test different null hypotheses; agreement is descriptive, not proof of validity.
  • Analytic and bootstrap intervals are pointwise 95% intervals, not multiplicity-adjusted. Their agreement compares whether they include the null; it is not a comparison with the Holm-adjusted p-value.
  • Bootstrap — Cell-stratified case bootstrap; fixed selected model; percentile intervals: 1000 requested, 1000 attempted, 1000 successful, 0 failed. Status: completed.
