What ran and why
  • Ordered categories are analyzed with a cumulative-logit model.
  • Selected model: ordinal_logit. Factors: Approach, Setting. Interactions included through order 2.
  • Comparisons are equally weighted differences or ratios of predicted means; 95% confidence intervals.
  • Factor tests evaluate model terms; comparisons evaluate predicted overall means. These can answer different questions for nonlinear or multipart models.
  • Differences subtract the second listed condition from the first; ratios divide the first by the second. The null value is 0 for differences and 1 for ratios. Interactions compare effects across settings.
  • Holm correction is applied across factor tests and within each factor's comparison family. Separate metrics are not corrected together; cross-metric findings are exploratory.
  • String conversion: 'high' → 2, 'medium' → 1, 'low' → 0
  • Ordered categories (lowest first): low, medium, high. Numeric codes express order; equal spacing is not assumed by the fitted ordinal model.
  • statsmodels fits; HC3 covariance for Gaussian/lognormal models and HC0 for other models. Nonlinear comparison intervals use the delta method.

Limitations and exclusions

  • DEMONSTRATION: synthetic data, not results from a real experiment.
  • Category order was recognized from conventional ordered labels.
  • Inference assumes independent runs and is conditional on the observed experimental inputs.
